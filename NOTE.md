# Pedal Panic: The Getaway Ride

**Idea:** A two-minute arcade bicycle chase. The rider picks a destination and helmet, completes a three-point safety check, then pedals away from a thief while collecting items and jumping obstacles. The game carries a light road-safety message.

**Who it might be useful for:** Kids and teens learning basic cycling safety habits, and casual players who want a quick arcade game in the browser.

**What I technically explored:** HTML5 Canvas pseudo-3D perspective projection, a requestAnimationFrame game loop with delta-time, procedurally drawn scenery (no image files), Web Audio API sound and music, localStorage leaderboard, responsive layout and fullscreen, and accessibility touches such as labels and mobile touch controls.

**Recent change:** The roadside scenery now scrolls more slowly than before (about 44% slower) to reduce eye strain. The rider's speed, obstacle speed and the last-minute speed-up are unchanged, and the scenery still speeds up in the final minute.

**With more time I would:** add difficulty levels, more obstacle types, a reduced-motion setting, and real recorded sound assets.
