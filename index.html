    Meet your ride: Metroline 700CC

    A sporty flat-bar city hybrid: upright enough for traffic visibility, with a quick, efficient feel on paved streets. The fictional frame is charcoal alloy with accents that follow your selected color theme.

    Frame & fitMedium 6061 aluminum fitness-hybrid frame; relaxed upright reach; rigid aluminum fork. Approx. 12.8 kg in game.
    Speed in the gameChase starts at 34 km/h and settles near 38. Hold CTRL to boost up to 64 km/h. In the final minute the chase speeds up: cruising near 48 km/h and boosting up to 74. Practice boost tops out at 54 km/h. Arcade values.
    Wheels & tires700C double-wall alloy rims, 38 mm puncture-resistant tires, reflective sidewalls. Both wheels spin while riding.
    Crankset & gearsSingle 40-tooth narrow-wide chainring, 170 mm crank arms, 8-speed 11–34T cassette, and a simple trigger shifter.
    PedalsWide alloy platform pedals with grippy pins; everyday shoes fit easily. Pedals and crank rotate as you pedal.
    BrakesMechanical disc brakes, 160 mm rotors front and rear, with textured brake levers on the flat bar.
    Carry & visibilityRear rack carries the zip bag; front LED lamp, rear red light, bell, full fenders, kickstand, and frame reflectors.
    How it movesThe frame stays solid. It leans slightly when steering; the wheels, chainring, crank, and pedals turn in motion.

    Ride feel: Keep a relaxed grip, look ahead, and steer smoothly. Brake before turns. In the game, use ← / → to steer, SPACE to hop, CTRL to boost, and ↑ to move up a lane.
